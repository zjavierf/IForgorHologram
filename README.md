## What it does



Ever lose track of where your hands were and it causes you to miss?



This mod creates holograms (or your real sabers) right where your hands were before you paused. Just line your real sabers back up with them before you unpause to not mess up your next swing.



---



## Features



- **Pause holograms:** Shows your exact hand position and rotation the second you hit pause.



- **Alignment guides:** Pick between using transparent real sabers or a custom guide laser beam to line your hands up.



- **Custom Saber Support:** Saber Factory and Custom Saber Lite sabers work with this mod. (**Reesabers** is not implemented right now)



- **Audio and haptics:** Gives you a clean sound cue and controller rumble the moment you hit the alignment spot.



- **Despawn animations:** Choose how the holograms clear out when you unpause—fade away, shrink down, or just disappear.



- **Custom colors:** Override the default game colors and set your own ghost colors.



- **UI settings:** Fully configurable through an in-game settings menu.



---



## Configurable Settings



Everything can be tweaked to your liking from the in-game settings menu:



| Setting | What it does | Choices / Range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mod Enabled** | Master switch for the mod. | True / False |
| **Hologram Mode** | Switches between transparent ghost look and standard saber colors. | On / Off |
| **Alignment Guide** | Changes how alignment feedback is shown. | None, Real Sabers, Guide Laser |
| **Despawn Animation** | Controls how the holograms exit on unpause. | None, Fade Away, Shrink |
| **Enable Audio Cue** | Plays a sound chime when you successfully align. | On / Off |
| **Audio Cue Volume** | Adjusts the volume percentage of the alignment sound. | 0% to 100% |
| **Enable Haptics** | Controller vibrates on alignment. | True / False |
| **Override Saber Colors** | Lets you use custom color schemes for the ghost sabers. | True / False |
| **Left / Right Saber Color** | Custom color pickers for each hand. | RGBA Picker |
