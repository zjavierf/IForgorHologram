# IForgorHologram

A Beat Saber mod that spawns ghost sabers when you pause, helping you check your hand position and grip before unpausing.

Built for Beat Saber 1.40.8.

---

## What It Does

When you pause a song, ghost sabers appear where your hands were. Line your real sabers up with them so you don't mess up your grip when you resume!

---

## Features

- **Pause Holograms:** Ghost sabers show your exact pre-pause hand position.
- **Alignment Guides:** Choose between real sabers (`RealSabers`) or a projection laser (`GuideLaser`) to help you line up.
- **Audio & Haptics:** Plays a sound and rumbles your controllers when your sabers are lined up correctly.
- **Despawn Effects:** Smooth animations (`FadeAway`, `Shrink`, or `None`) when you resume playing.
- **Custom Colors:** Give your ghost sabers custom colors instead of the game defaults.
- **In-Game Settings:** Fully configurable via the BSML settings menu.

---

## Settings Guide

- **Mod Enabled:** Turn the mod on or off.
- **Hologram Mode:** Switch between a transparent grid hologram look and solid colors.
- **Alignment Guide:** Pick how you want alignment shown (`None`, `RealSabers`, or `GuideLaser`).
- **Despawn Animation:** Choose how the holograms disappear on unpause (`None`, `FadeAway`, `Shrink`).
- **Override Saber Colors:** Enable custom colors for ghost sabers.
- **Left / Right Saber Color:** Pick custom colors for each hand.
- **Reset Colors:** Reset ghost colors back to default.
- **Enable Audio Cue:** Turn the alignment chime sound on or off.
- **Audio Cue Volume:** Adjust the volume of the alignment sound.
